IWhy Stars

The future is in our Stars

Stars Foundation mentors Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander girls and young women in schools across Australia. Full-time Mentors run the Stars Room, a culturally safe, warm place at school where girls build the confidence, self-esteem and life skills to stay engaged, finish Year 12 and move into work or further study.

It is a program built on strong, trusting relationships. Which, if you think about it, is what a good practice community is too.
